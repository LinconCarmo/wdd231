Santos Futebol Clube Site Plan

Site Name

Santos Futebol Clube Fan Website

This website is dedicated to Santos Futebol Clube, one of Brazil's most successful football clubs. The name clearly reflects the purpose of the site and allows visitors to immediately understand the content they will find.

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to provide information about Santos Futebol Clube, including its history, legendary players, trophies, and club achievements. Visitors will also be able to contact the website through a simple contact form.

Scenarios

  • Where can I learn about the history of Santos Futebol Clube?
  • Who are the greatest players in Santos FC history?
  • What are the club's major achievements and trophies?
